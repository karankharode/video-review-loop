# BROLL — v2 Plantymers

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920. 10 shots, 50 seconds total.
**No text, no logos, no people's faces in any generated shot.** Hands only.

---

## Global continuity spec — apply to every prompt

Paste this block into every generation. It is what makes ten clips read as one film.

**Palette:** warm neutral base — bone white, pale bench grey, raw linen. Two accents only: **maize yellow** (the organic side) and **cold sea-grey** (the petroleum side). No teal-orange grading, no saturated blues, no neon.

**Lighting:** single large soft key from frame left, low and slightly raking, as if from a north-facing window. Deep but not black shadows on the right. Consistent colour temperature ~4800K throughout. No hard speculars, no lens flares.

**Camera language:** locked-off or very slow dolly only. Maximum movement is a 5–8% push or pull across a whole shot. Shallow depth of field (equivalent 50mm–100mm macro, f/2.0–f/2.8). Every shot is either a tabletop macro or a tabletop wide — the viewer never leaves the height of a bench.

**Texture:** subtle 35mm grain, very slight halation on highlights. No digital sharpening.

**Grade shift (the "then vs now" spine):** shots 2 and 3 are desaturated ~30% and pushed one step cooler. Shot 4 snaps back to full warmth — that colour snap *is* the "now". Everything after stays warm. Shot 10 dims down toward soil brown.
