If entry-level jobs are shrinking... what are you practising for?

Gartner asked a hundred and ten HR heads worldwide. Twenty-two percent said at least one leader had stopped hiring freshers, because of AI.

Then Drexel surveyed six hundred employers on what they want from graduates. Communication ranked above technical skill. The technical part, they said, they'd train.

Fewer first jobs. That's the market, not you. Three fixes.

Three. Say your answers out loud. Reading them silently is not practice.

Two. Record one interview answer a day. SAI scores what you said.

One. Practise the unexpected follow-up. That's what you're practising for. Save this.
