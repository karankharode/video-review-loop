## Continuity bible — apply to EVERY prompt

> **Palette:** deep blue-green forest, warm amber low sun, cold cyan for anything laser or scan-derived. No other hues. Cyan appears only from shot 5 onward and is the only synthetic colour in the film.
> **Light:** single low sun, roughly 20° above the horizon, coming from frame-left throughout. Heavy atmospheric haze in the valleys. The sun sits lower shot by shot; by shot 8 it is at the horizon.
> **Camera language:** slow, heavy, deliberate. Aerial drone or crane moves only — no handheld, no whip pans, no snap zooms. Every move continues in the same direction it started. Shallow-to-medium depth, subtle anamorphic flare on the sun.
> **Grain and format:** filmic 35mm grain, gentle halation, 2.39-crushed blacks lifted very slightly. Documentary realism, not fantasy illustration.
> **Era:** the human-made structures are earth only — ditches, embankments, cleared geometry. No stone masonry, no pyramids, no statues, no gold. Nothing metallic. This is a civilisation that built with soil.
> **No people on camera at any point. No text, no captions, no UI, no watermarks, no lens dirt.**
