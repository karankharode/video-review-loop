Lasers just found three hundred and ninety-six lost monuments under the Amazon rainforest.

You were taught Amazonia was empty. Too poor to farm... nothing built to last.

A University of Helsinki team published the survey. They flew lasers over four thousand five hundred square kilometres of rainforest. Some beams slipped through... mapping the ground below.

Four hundred and thirty-two earthworks. Three hundred and ninety-six of them... never recorded.

Built six hundred BC. Abandoned by nine fifty AD. Nobody knows why.

They estimate twenty thousand more are down there... The forest grew over them.
