**TTS notes**

- `ee-coli` is a deliberate phonetic spelling for "E. coli". Do not "correct" it back to `E. coli` — most engines read that as the letter E followed by a pause, or as "eee-see-oh-ell-eye".
- The "..." before "But the break follows the brains" is the single most important pause in the render. It is mandated, not optional, and it is already inside the 30.0s budget.
- Every other "..." is a ~0.3–0.4s hold. Do not lengthen them to fill time; there is no spare time.
