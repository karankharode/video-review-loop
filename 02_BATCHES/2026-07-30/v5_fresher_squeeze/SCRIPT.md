## The one thing the viewer leaves with

The reason they aren't hearing back is a shrinking market, not a personal failure — and the only part of it they actually control is the one conversation they get, which is worth rehearsing out loud.

---

## Hook — first 3 seconds

**Spoken (10 words across 0–6s; first 8 land inside 3s):**
> "You've applied everywhere. You've started thinking it's you. It's not."

**On-screen text at 0s (≤6 words):**
`IT'S NOT YOU`

### Alternates considered

| # | Spoken hook | On-screen | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | "Eighty percent of fresher IT jobs are gone. Not exaggerating." | `80% GONE` | Strong Astonish energy, but it opens on the market instead of on the viewer. Register is Recognise — the first words have to be about them, not about an industry. |
| C | "You got the offer letter. Seven months later, still nothing." | `STILL WAITING` | Excellent callout, but it only speaks to the minority who already have an offer. Excludes most of the audience at second one. Kept as the row-3 beat instead. |

**Why A won:** it names the private thought — *"maybe I'm the problem"* — that the viewer has never said out loud, and then contradicts it inside three seconds. That's the Recognise register doing its actual job, and it removes shame before a single statistic lands. It also sets up the last line as a callback.

## Caption

Fresher hiring in Indian IT fell from roughly 6,00,000 in FY22 to about 1,20,000 in FY25 — an 80% drop, per Xpheno. Offer letters are being issued and joining dates pushed by months. None of that is about you. What is about you: the one conversation you get, and whether you've ever heard yourself answer out loud. Pick three questions you know are coming. Record yourself. Listen back once — it's uncomfortable, and it works.
