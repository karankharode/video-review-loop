# v5 — The Fresher Squeeze — RENDER NOTES

Shoot day required. This is the only piece in the batch with a human on camera, and that is the asset — every choice below protects the impression that a real person is telling you something true.

---

## Shoot

**Framing**
- 9:16, 4K if the phone allows, 24fps (25fps if the room lights flicker) — 24fps reads as "filmed", 60fps reads as "vlog".
- Two setups, and you cut between them at the **23.3s** retention beat (it moved earlier than 26s when the script was cut to length — see `SCRIPT.md`):
  - **Setup A (0–23.3s):** medium shot. Karan from mid-chest up, positioned slightly off-centre — head in the upper third, negative space to his left. Eyeline directly into lens.
  - **Setup B (23.3–60s):** tighter. Shoulders up. Different angle by roughly 30 degrees, and either a different wall behind him or the same wall from a visibly different distance. The change must be noticeable in half a second — that's what makes it a pattern break.
- Lens: phone's 2x / telephoto if available. The ultra-wide distorts faces at this distance and instantly reads as amateur.
- Camera at eye level. Not below — looking up at someone delivering "it's not you" is the wrong power dynamic for this script.
- Leave 8–10% headroom for caption burn-in at the bottom third.

**Location**
- One room, real, lived-in. A desk corner with depth behind him — a doorway, a shelf, a window several feet back. Not a flat wall pressed against his head, and not a "content studio" set with LED panels and plants.
- Some visible depth/bokeh behind him sells the 2x lens. Avoid anything with text on it in the background — it steals reads.
- Quiet room. No fan, no AC compressor cycling. Shoot with a lav or a phone-mounted mic within 30cm — this script has four deliberate silences (~3s in total, budgeted into the 60s) and room hiss will ruin all of them.

**Lighting**
- Soft key from a window at roughly 45 degrees, camera-left, with a white wall or a cheap bounce card camera-right to lift the shadow side. Nothing dramatic.
- Do not backlight, do not colour-gel, do not ring-light. A ring light's catchlight is the single most recognisable "this is an ad" signal on Reels.
- If shooting after dark, one softbox or a bedsheet-diffused lamp at the same 45. Keep it flat and honest.

**Wardrobe consistency**
- Plain solid mid-tone shirt or crew tee. No logos, no stripes, no fine patterns (they moiré on phone sensors), no pure white (blows out), no pure black (crushes).
- **This is a batch — match the wardrobe to whatever Karan wears in any other on-camera piece shot the same week, or shoot them all in the same session.** If it can't match, it must clearly *not* match, so it reads as a different day rather than a continuity error.
- No watch or jewellery that catches the key light and flares on every gesture.
- Shoot Setup A and Setup B back to back without changing anything. The angle changes; he does not.

**Coverage to grab before wrapping**
- Three full takes of the whole script.
- Then five isolated takes of the line "It's a market condition. Not a character flaw." — different pacing on each. You will use one of these, not the version from the full take.
- 10s of Karan sitting still, not talking, eyes on lens. Insurance for the final hold.

---

## Music

- **Brief:** sparse, low, single sustained pad with a slow pulse. Something that sits under speech without competing — think a held synth note with a soft filter movement, no melody, no drums for the first 24s.
- Bring in a soft kick or subtle rhythmic element only from 39.1s ("So the filter moved"), rising slightly into the ending. That lift is the only emotional signal the music is allowed to make.
- **Mix at −22 to −26 LUFS under the VO.** It should be felt, not heard. If you can hum it back after one watch, it's too loud and too melodic.
- **Duck the music to near-silence for 23.3–32.7s.** The "market condition, not a character flaw" line plays almost dry. That drop is more powerful than any swell.
- No trending audio. No lo-fi hip-hop. No inspirational piano. This topic is people's livelihoods — sentimental music makes it feel exploitative.
- Source: Epidemic Sound "ambient / tension - minimal" or Artlist "underscore". Keep the licence receipt in the batch folder.

---

## Captions

- Burn-in, always — assume sound off on first view.
- Style: bold sans (Inter Bold / Montserrat SemiBold), white with a subtle dark drop-shadow or a 60%-opacity dark pill behind. No outline stroke, no rainbow, no word-by-word bounce animation.
- **Two to four words per card, cutting on the natural speech beat.** The pauses in the performance script must be visible in the captions — when Karan pauses, the card holds or clears. Don't let the caption run ahead of the voice.
- Highlight colour on exactly five phrases and nowhere else: `IT'S NOT YOU`, `80%`, `SEVEN MONTHS`, `NOT A CHARACTER FLAW`, `ONE CONVERSATION`. One accent colour from the SAI palette. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.
- Numbers in Indian format on screen: `6,00,000` / `1,20,000`.
- Position in the lower third but above the platform UI safe zone — roughly 18–22% up from the bottom.

## Edit

- **First frame must be motion.** Start on the push-in already moving with Karan mid-word. Zero frames of static setup. No logo sting, no intro card.
- Cut on his breaths, not on the beat of the music. This is a talking piece — musical cutting makes it feel produced.
- **Do not cut away between 23.3s and 32.7s.** Locked. This is stated again here because it is the note most likely to be overridden in the edit. The window sits earlier than in the previous draft only because the rows before it were shortened; its length and its absoluteness are unchanged.
- **The final beat is on Karan.** B6 is out by 55.6s; the last eight words and the one-second dead hold are on his face. Do not extend the voice-memo cutaway to cover the ending — `BROLL.md` is authoritative on all cutaway timing and `SCRIPT.md` has been conformed to it.
- Micro-zoom (2–4% over 5–6s) on the sustained talking-head sections to keep motion alive. Alternate direction so it doesn't creep in one way for the whole video.
- Trim breaths at the front of lines, but **keep the four deliberate pauses at full length** (before "It's not.", before "When it dies in silence", between "market condition." and "Not a character flaw.", and the one-second hold on the final frame). The script is 130 words = 53.8s at 145 wpm, so the pauses and the hold are already paid for and the piece still lands at ~57.8s. If it comes out at 58s, that is correct. If someone tightens the pauses to "fill" 60s, put them back.
- Grade: neutral, slightly desaturated, mild contrast. No teal-and-orange, no film-burn overlays. The b-roll and the talking head must match — grade B2 and B4 toward the skin tones, not the other way around.
- Export: H.264, 9:16, 1080×1920, high bitrate, −14 LUFS integrated on the master.
- **Final QA before publish:** watch it once on mute. If it reads as a motivational video rather than a straight account of a market, re-cut.

## Thumbnail / cover

**Concept:** Karan mid-sentence in the Setup B tighter frame — not smiling, not posed, mouth slightly open as if caught mid-word. Shot from the "market condition" take, which will have the right face on it. Frame him hard to the right of the vertical cover. Left third stays empty for text.

Behind him, low-opacity and heavily blurred so it's texture rather than content, the B2 counter graphic mid-roll — the shape of a big number falling, illegible but readable as *a number going down*.

Grade cooler than the video by a touch. No border, no arrow, no circled face, no shocked expression.

**Thumbnail text (≤4 words):**
> `IT'S NOT YOU`

Set large, left-aligned, stacked over three lines, in the brand's heaviest weight. `NOT` in the accent colour, everything else white. Must be legible at 120px wide — check it at thumbnail size on an actual phone before locking.

**Alternate if a face-forward cover tests badly:** `FEWER CHAIRS` over the B4 empty-corridor still, no face. Keep it as a swap option for the YouTube A/B test slot.
