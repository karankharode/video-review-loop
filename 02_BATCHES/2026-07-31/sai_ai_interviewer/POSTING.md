## Posting notes

- **First 3 seconds are the whole thing.** Don't add an intro card, logo sting, or "hey guys" — the hook is the first frame.
- **Post with sound on by default.** The VO carries the argument; the captions are reinforcement, not a substitute.
- **Best slot for this audience** (Indian students/freshers): weekday evenings, roughly 7–10pm IST, when placement anxiety is highest.
- **Pin a first comment** with the hashtags and the Fortune link. Sources in the comment reads as confident, sources in the caption reads as defensive.
- **Expect a "so it IS biased?" reply.** Honest answer, and worth having ready: yes, paralinguistic scoring carries documented bias risk around accents and second-language English — that's a separate video, and it's the reason HireVue dropped the facial layer in the first place.

## Do not claim

- Don't generalise beyond HireVue in replies. The sourced facts are about HireVue specifically; other vendors differ.
- Don't say SAI "beats" or "cracks" AI interviews. Sayable: voice practice, real-time feedback on spoken answers, interview scoring.
- No fluency-in-N-days promises, no invented user numbers.
