# Performance script — v1 Waterjet Through Rock

**This is the on-camera script. Read down the left column. The right column is direction, not words.**

Total 50.0s. 114 spoken words at 150 wpm = 45.6s of speech, ~2.2s of pauses, 1.2s dead hold at the end.

Do **not** speed up to fit. If you're running long, the fix is in `SCRIPT.md`'s cut list, not in your delivery.

---

## Setups

You need **three camera setups**. Not five — three, reused.

| Setup | Framing | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **A — wide-ish, energy** | Mid-chest, slight handheld drift, room visible | Hook, the turn |
| **B — tight, flat** | Head-and-shoulders, locked off, dead centre | The reveal, the locked beat |
| **C — tighter, warm** | Closer than B, small lean-in | The close |

Shoot every line in all three if you have time. It costs nothing on the day and it gives the edit somewhere to go.

---

## The lines

| # | Say this | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Solid-state batteries have been two years away for ten years." | **Setup A.** Already talking as the frame settles. Dry, slightly amused — you're in on the joke with them. Not sarcastic. |
| 2 | "Everyone assumed the holdup was chemistry." | Flatter. This is the setup you're about to knock down, so don't sell it. |
| 3 | "It's not chemistry. It's pressure." | **Setup B. Cut here.** Beat *before* you say it. Land "pressure" and stop. This is the reveal. |
| 4 | "Inside one, lithium has to cross a ceramic wall." | Explaining now, not arguing. Slower. |
| 5 | "That ceramic is harder than the lithium touching it." | Same register. Let the graphic do the work. |
| 6 | "So the lithium should lose." | Slight upward inflection — you're setting a trap. |
| 7 | **"It doesn't."** | **Setup B, locked. Beat before AND after.** Do not move your head. This is the two-word turn the whole piece hangs on. Give it a full second of silence on the back end. |
| 8 | "Researchers froze one mid-failure and looked." | Back to normal pace. Matter-of-fact. |
| 9 | "The lithium finds a crack already there, fills it, and can't escape." | The word that matters is **"already"** — the crack was there first. Lean on it slightly. |
| 10 | "Pressure builds. The ceramic splits from the inside." | Two short sentences. Full stop between them. Don't run them together. |
| 11 | "Soft metal splitting hard stone. Like a waterjet through rock." | **The most quotable line in the piece.** Slow down about 15%. Let "waterjet" land clean. |
| 12 | "That's the short circuit." | Clipped. Closing a loop. |
| 13 | "Not new chemistry. Better engineering." | **Setup A. Cut here.** Energy comes back up. This is the turn toward the payoff. |
| 14 | "Tougher ceramic. Coatings. And tiny deliberate voids that steer the crack somewhere harmless." | Three items — put an audible gap after "ceramic" and after "Coatings" so the graphics can land one per item. |
| 15 | "The problem was never the recipe. It was the pressure." | **Setup C.** Quieter than everything before it. "Pressure" is the bookend — same weight you gave it in line 3. |
| — | *(say nothing)* | **Hold 1.2s.** Stay in frame. Don't smile, don't nod, don't reach for the camera. The dead second is the beat that makes it feel finished. |

---

## Four things that will wreck this take

1. **Rushing line 7.** "It doesn't." is 0.8s of speech surrounded by 1.4s of silence. Every instinct will tell you to fill that. Don't.
2. **Selling line 2.** If you perform "Everyone assumed the holdup was chemistry" with conviction, the reveal has nothing to push against.
3. **Smiling into the hold.** The last 1.2s is the frame people screenshot. Neutral.
4. **Getting faster as you go.** Overruns concentrate at the end. If anything, the last three lines should be your slowest.

---

## Shooting notes

- Record **room tone** for 30s before you leave. The edit will need it under the pauses.
- Frame with headroom for a **caption bar in the bottom 250px** — Instagram's UI sits there and captions are placed above it.
- The bottom panel of the Kallaway layout is roughly the lower 45% of frame, so **you will be cropped**. Keep your face in the upper-middle of your own frame, not centred.
- Shoot 4K if you can. The edit crops in, and the punch-in on entry eats resolution.

## Scratch guide track

`AVATAR_SCRIPT.md` holds the same 114 words with no formatting. It exists so the pipeline can generate a **scratch VO** (`voice.wav`) that times the placeholder cut before you shoot.

That scratch track is a **stopwatch, not a performance.** It gets replaced by your recorded audio. Don't tune your delivery to match it — if anything it will be slightly faster than you want to be.
