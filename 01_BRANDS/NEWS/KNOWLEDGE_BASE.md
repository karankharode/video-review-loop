## Promotion rules

| Stage | Requirement | Effect on the pipeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | Logged in `FEEDBACK_LEDGER.md` | None. Just recorded. |
| **Candidate** | Same signal seen **2×** | Listed below. Pipeline may consider it. Not binding. |
| **Rule** | Same signal seen **3×**, no contradiction | Binding. Pipeline must follow it. |
| **Retired** | A rule contradicted **2×** | Moved to Retired with the date and the contradiction. |

Posted-performance data outweighs review-stage opinion. A pattern Karan disliked in review but that performed well when posted gets flagged as a **conflict**, not silently overwritten — conflicts go in their own section for a human call.
