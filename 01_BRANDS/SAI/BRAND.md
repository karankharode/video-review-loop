# Track: SAI

SAI (Skill Adaptive Intelligence) — AI voice confidence and communication coach for Indian students and professionals.

**Authoritative voice + pillar + format spec lives in the `sai-script-engine` skill.** Read it before writing anything on this track. This file only covers what's specific to the news-driven loop.

---

## How news topics enter this track

A news story qualifies for SAI only if it can be landed on one of these bridges without strain. If the bridge feels forced, the topic belongs on the NEWS track instead.

| Bridge | Example news → SAI angle |
|---|---|
| **Hiring / placements shift** | "Company X drops degree requirement" → what they screen for instead, and how you say it out loud |
| **AI at work** | "AI now does Y" → the part of your job that's now purely communication |
| **Exam / education policy** | NCERT, CBSE, NEP changes → what actually changes for a student's viva or interview |
| **Workplace culture story** | Layoffs, RTO, manager-behaviour stories → the phrase you needed in that conversation |
| **Founder/tech news** | Model launches, funding, tooling → build-in-public reaction, what it means for building SAI |

**Hard rule:** the news is the hook, not the content. If the viewer only learns news and nothing about speaking, it failed.

---

## Audience default

Indian students (school + college) and young professionals. Second person, always. Indian context words by default: viva, placements, GD, HR round, the interview, your manager, the class.

## Product claims — verify before shipping

Sayable: voice practice, real-time feedback on spoken answers, adaptive journey, NCERT quiz-show lessons, interview scoring, workplace roleplay.

Never: grammar teaching claims, fluency-in-N-days promises, invented user counts or ratings.
